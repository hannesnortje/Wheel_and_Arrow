{{ t.app_title }} is a booklet and a small app on personal leadership and goal setting by {{ t.author }}. The booklet takes you in six short chapters through success, the inner foundation, the Wheel of Life and setting goals. The three tools – Wheel of Life, Goal Planning Worksheet and Whole-Life Goal Plan – help you put what you read into practice straight away.

Your data

Everything you enter in the tools is stored only in the browser on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. Use “Download my data” to keep a backup file or to move your entries to another device.
