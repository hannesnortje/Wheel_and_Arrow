About

The Wheel and the Arrow is a booklet and a small app on personal leadership and goal setting by Dr. Johannes (Hannes) A. Nortjé. The booklet takes you in six short chapters through success, the inner foundation, the Wheel of Life and setting goals. The three tools – Wheel of Life, Goal Planning Worksheet and Whole-Life Goal Plan – help you put what you read into practice straight away.

Your data

Everything you enter in the tools is stored only in the browser on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. Use “Download my data” to keep a backup file or to move your entries to another device.

Sources and thanks

The definition of success in the booklet is by Paul J. Meyer and is quoted with attribution. Inspired by the personal-development tradition, including the work of Paul J. Meyer. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Leadership Management International.

The study on written goals: Gail Matthews, Dominican University of California, presented in 2007 at the 87th Convention of the Western Psychological Association. The often-quoted “Yale study” (3%) never took place.

Bible passages: own translation from the Hebrew and Greek by Dr. Johannes A. Nortjé.
