Whole-Life Goal Plan

My Whole-Life Goal Plan

A balanced framework for setting and tracking goals across the areas of my life

Auto-saves in this browser

(My Guiding Principle)

In a sentence or two, capture what you most want your life to stand for and become. Your mission statement forms the guardrails and boundaries within which all your goals must fit and move.

Five habits that make it work

  1. Write it down. Goals kept only in your head stay vague. Putting them into plain words sharpens them and turns an intention into a real commitment.
  2. Revisit it often. Read through your plan regularly — first thing in the morning works well — so it keeps steering your attention and daily choices.
  3. Keep it balanced. Try to keep at least one live goal in every area. Strong progress in one part of life rarely makes up for neglecting another.
  4. Break down the big ones. For your top priorities, spell out the next concrete action, the obstacles you expect, and how you plan to handle them.
  5. Give each a deadline. A target date creates focus and accountability. If life changes, move the date — adjusting a timeline is healthy; quietly abandoning the goal is not.

The areas of my life

Choose a goal type and priority, write the goal so it is specific and measurable, add a target date, and update the status as you go. Add as many lines as you need.

Priority: 1 = highest, 5 = lowest · Short-term: under 1 year · Medium-term: 1–3 years · Long-term: 3+ years
